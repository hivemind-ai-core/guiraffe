# UX Research

Read the [UX research guide](../../../docs/ui-ux/ux-research.md).

1. Identify the decision, target participants, context of use and evidence that could change the decision.
2. Match the method to the question. Use neutral tasks with realistic starting states and success criteria. Include relevant access needs and document consent and data retention.
3. Report observed behavior, interventions and outcomes separately from interpretation. If the work is a study plan, leave observations pending.

Deliver a research brief, recruitment criteria, tasks, observation template, synthesis or analysis plan, and the decision the evidence supports.
